Directions for the homepage

Each one is a different idea of what esmrsky is, built on the real list of 74 sites, and each works on a phone. The map is the newest, built after the first four. The live homepage at esmrsky.github.io is unchanged.

  1. Direction 5 · newestThe mapEvery site as a card with its own icon and one plain line on what you’ll find, grouped into six colored topics. Filter by topic or format at the top, or search.
  2. Direction 1GalleryThe site as a museum. Each room opens with a painting the studies already use, and every study hangs beside it as a wall label. Dark warm walls, a classic serif.
  3. Direction 2ContentsThe site as one book. A title page, then a numbered contents in seven parts, with each study’s first sentence under its name. Paper, ink and one red.
  4. Direction 3QuestionThe site as a place to bring something. Eleven plain lines like “I keep doing the thing I hate.” Each opens only the studies written for it. Everything else is in an A-to-Z below.
  5. Direction 4Two BooksThe site as an argument. Scripture on one page, the brain and body on the other, and the eight claims they share running down the binding between them.

These are first drafts of an idea, not finished pages: English only, no theme toggle (each follows your device’s light or dark setting), and the wording of the questions and the side each study sits on are first passes.
