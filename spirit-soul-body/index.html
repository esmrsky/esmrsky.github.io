In everyday life

One ordinary moment, three layers

This isn't abstract. All three are running right now, in you, at the same time. Here's what that looks like on a Tuesday night.

10:40 PMA short email from your manager lands on your phone: "Can we talk tomorrow morning." No subject. No smiley. Nothing else.

Within 2 seconds · Body

The alarm fires first

Your chest tightens. Jaw sets. Heart rate climbs. Adrenaline and cortisol are already moving before a single thought has finished forming. Nobody chose this. This is hardware doing exactly what it was built to do.

Seconds 3 to 300 · Soul

The mind writes the story

Now the scripts start. What did I do? Is this about last week? Am I being managed out? The emotions pile in behind the thoughts. And the will leans toward a defensive reply at 10:47pm — the one you'd delete by morning.

Underneath all of it · Spirit

The quiet that doesn't argue

And beneath the noise, something steadier: you are not what this job says you are. It isn't loud. It doesn't compete for airtime, and it never uses urgency. It simply holds still and waits to be listened to.

All three are you. None of them is the enemy. The question is never which one is real — it's which one is driving.

A picture for it

The house you live in

If the rings feel too abstract, try a house.

BODY

The house itself. Walls, wiring, plumbing. It has real limits — it needs feeding, sleep, and maintenance, and it complains loudly when it doesn't get them.

SOUL

The person living in it. How it's furnished, what music is playing, which rooms are a mess, who gets a key. Your tastes, moods, memories, decisions.

SPIRIT

The one room with a window facing out. The only room in the house where you meet Someone who isn't you.

Which is why the failures come in three shapes: a house left to rot until the resident can't think straight — a house redecorated forever with the window painted shut — or a person who sits in that room daily, and slowly starts keeping the whole house differently.

You can't pray well on four hours' sleep, and you can't sleep your way to communion with God. Both are true at once.

A word on method

Does science explain God away?

No — it explains the mechanism, not the meaning. A scan can show what happens in you when you pray or forgive. It can't tell you whether God is on the other end. Science and Scripture answer different questions.

Science asks

How?

Cortisol, neurons, networks — the mechanism. What happens, and how it works.

Scripture answers

Who & why?

Who made it, and for what — the meaning a mechanism can never hold.

Knowing how the watch works has never disproved the watchmaker. The clearer the design, the more there is to marvel at — the mechanism points to a Maker.

"Clearly seen… from what has been made" · Romans 1:20 · NIV

Soul · Faculties

Mind, will & emotion

The soul has three faculties — the mind that knows, the will that chooses, and the emotions that respond.

They're made to work in concert: know what's true, feel its weight, choose accordingly. Trouble comes when one seizes the wheel — feeling that drags the will, or a cold mind that loves nothing.

"Love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your mind and with all your strength."

Renewing the mind, literally

"Do not conform to the pattern of this world, but be transformed by the renewing of your mind."

Romans 12:2 · NIV

The adult brain isn't fixed — it's plastic. Every repeated thought strengthens a neural pathway. The brain rewires around what we dwell on.

Renewal isn't only a feeling. What you rehearse, you become.

The soul talking — mind, will and emotion at work

Rehearsing an argument in the shower — four hours after it ended.
Reading a two-word text as an insult, then finding out they were just driving.
Knowing exactly what you should apologise for, and not wanting to.
The pit in your stomach before you speak, that vanishes the second you start.
Scrolling someone's life at midnight and quietly resenting your own.
Agreeing to something you didn't want, because saying no felt too expensive.

None of this is sin by itself — it's the self, working. It becomes trouble when one faculty grabs the wheel: feeling that drags the will, or a cold mind that loves nothing.

The spirit talking — quieter, and easy to miss

The small check just before you send the message you'd regret. You felt it. You sent it anyway.
Peace in a hospital corridor that makes no sense given the circumstances.
One line in a sermon that wasn't about you, landing like it was written for you.
The pull to call someone you hadn't thought about in months — and finding out why.
Getting exactly what you wanted and feeling strangely unfed by it.
Discomfort after a joke at someone's expense that everyone else laughed at.

Notice the pattern: it rarely shouts, it never panics, and it doesn't need you to decide this second. Noise from the body and soul can drown it out entirely — which is why stillness isn't a luxury.

Spirit · Intuition

Gut feelings & the listening heart

Scripture locates discernment in the heart — and even the gut. Paul's word for compassion, splagchna, literally means the bowels.

The body agrees. The gut and heart carry their own neural networks; most of the vagus nerve runs upward, body to brain.

Enteric nervous system · GershonHeart coherence · HeartMath

A gut feeling is real data — but data, not a verdict. A signal to weigh, not a voice to obey.

Vagus nerve ≈ 80% of fibers carry signal ↑ upward (body → brain)

The most common mix-up

Soul feeling, or Spirit witness?

This is the confusion that costs people years — jobs taken, relationships started, money given, all on a strong feeling that turned out to be just a strong feeling.

Hebrews says the Word is sharp enough to divide soul and spirit — which implies they look alike from the inside. They do. But over time they behave very differently.

The cheapest test in the world: wait a week and tell one wise person. Soul excitement usually can't survive either. A genuine witness of the Spirit almost always can.

And note what this isn't saying — emotion is not the enemy. God made it. Jesus wept, and was moved with compassion. The point isn't to feel less; it's to stop putting feelings in a chair they were never built to sit in.

Try it on

"Which layer is this?"

Here's where the model earns its keep. Six things people actually say — and the questions to ask in order, outside in, before reaching for the spiritual answer.

Tap any one to open it.

BODYHow much have you slept this week? Distance from God and sleep debt produce nearly identical symptoms — flatness, irritability, no appetite for prayer.
SOULIs there anger or grief you haven't named? Unprocessed emotion muffles everything, not just God.
SPIRITIs there a yes you're avoiding? Sometimes the silence is the sound of a question you've stopped answering.

Check in that order. Sometimes the most spiritual thing available to you is going to bed.

BODYCaffeine, screens, no daylight, a threat system stuck in the "on" position. Worry has a chemistry, and you can change some of the inputs today.
SOULA story you keep rehearsing. Every replay deepens the groove — that's the same neuroplasticity that renews a mind, running in reverse.
SPIRIT"Cast it on him" is an action, not a mood. Say the specific thing out loud to God, by name.

Note the overlap: naming a worry to God is also affect labeling. One act, and every layer benefits.

SOULCertainty is a feeling, and feelings are soul. Strong conviction and strong emotion are indistinguishable in the moment — that's not a personal failing, it's the design.
SPIRITA genuine witness doesn't evaporate when you slow down. Give it a week and a wise friend before you act on it.

Being wrong about this doesn't mean you can't hear God. It means you're a person with a soul — which is exactly why Scripture tells us to test things.

BODYHabit loops live in the body — cue, routine, reward. Willpower alone is a weak lever against a well-worn path. Change what's within reach, when you're tired, and where you are at 11pm.
SOULWhat is the behaviour actually giving you? Comfort, numbness, control, revenge? Name the payoff and it loses some of its grip.
SPIRITPaul lands here in Romans 7 and doesn't end in self-hatred — he ends in rescue. Grace, then effort. Not effort in the hope of grace.

Change the inputs, not just the intentions. Both layers are real; only one of them responds to being scolded.

SOULFeeling is soul. It runs on sleep, hormones, weather, and what happened at work — and it is a famously unreliable narrator of what's actually true.
SPIRITCommunion is spirit. The two run on different clocks. Plenty of faithful prayer has felt like nothing at all at the time.

Don't measure the spirit with the soul's instruments. Show up dry. It counts.

BODYComfortable and depleted is a real combination. Chronic low-grade stress doesn't need a crisis to wear you down.
SOULA life arranged around what you wanted at twenty-two, still running long after you changed.
SPIRITThe innermost room was built for Someone, and it stays hungry whether or not the rest of the house is comfortable. This is the ache nothing else fits.

The hollowness at the top of the ladder is not a malfunction. It's the one part of you that was never going to be satisfied by the climb.
