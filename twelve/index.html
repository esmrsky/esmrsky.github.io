Method and boundaries

Scripture first. Frameworks second.

Twelve distinguishes explicit lineage and tribal association from story resonance. APEST, redemptive gifts, and modern archetypes are translation layers—not biblical claims of identity.

Strong

Directly anchored

Tribal blessing, lineage, association, or an organizing claim supported by multiple canonical anchors.

Partial

Meaningful overlap

A framework illuminates part of the pattern, but the two systems are not identical.

Interpretive

Useful translation

A modern or ministry vocabulary helps name the pattern; it should be held lightly and tested by fruit.

No clean map

Honest restraint

When the evidence does not support a neat correspondence, the atlas leaves the category open.
